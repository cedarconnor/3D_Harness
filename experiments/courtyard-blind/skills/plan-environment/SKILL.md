description: Plan this packet's bounded environment with shared assets, proportions and fixed review views.
---

Inspect the assigned starter, then write a short asset list and task plan under output/. Separate mood guidance from dimensional evidence. Inferred construction details should support the brief rather than change its requirements. Work from large silhouette and composition toward secondary detail. Six to eight meaningful asset families should cover architecture, the hero prop and supporting environment; repeated placements should reuse a definition.

Make shared materials deliberate: coherent palette, physical texture scale, roughness, color spaces and projection. Plan three fixed view checks and a checklist that would reject a plausible wrong result, including meaningful geometry, the hero dimension and shared stone use. Keep aesthetic decisions distinct from hard requirements.

For revision, identify permitted geometry/material changes and preservation locks before editing. Use only the supplied files and tools. Record decisions and unresolved work for continuation; avoid adding requirements or rebuilding unrelated assets.
