# A working potting shed after rain

Build a convincing, realistically detailed environment from the supplied geometry-free starter. This is an original compact brick potting shed beside a garden, viewed at human height. The intended finish is an architectural/environment still that rewards looking closely: believable scale, construction, textures and plant silhouettes. Avoid a miniature presentation, oversized bevels, toy proportions and uniform procedural noise. Do not imitate a prior courtyard.

The starter fixes three cameras, illumination, color management and render settings for fair comparison. Keep those records unchanged in the saved result. Compose within those views; inspect them early. Site ground should extend far enough to avoid a visible diorama base or void edge. Main façade near Y=1.4 spans roughly X=-3 to 3, height about 3 m; a return and a simple pitched/sloped roof imply a complete structure. These are authored design dimensions, not measurements recovered from the photographs. The exterior is the deliverable; a full interior is not required.

Place a working potting bench centered near (-1.2, 0.25), tabletop upper surface Z=0.90. Its complete top must be one mesh object ext.bench.top with measured world dimensions 1.80 × 0.65 × 0.06 m, tolerance 0.01 m. Separate planks may be disconnected components in that mesh. Give it credible legs, stretchers, end grain, joins and restrained worn edges. Other bench structure uses ext.bench.* IDs; loose tools, pots and supplies must use separate ext.prop.* IDs.

The front door, ext.building.door, is near X=1.35 and is approximately 0.95 m wide × 2.1 m tall. Use a real wall opening, jambs, lintel and threshold rather than a rectangle floating on an intact wall. ext.building.facade is the main wall mesh, possibly disconnected around openings. A window near the bench, sill, gutter/downpipe or flashing, credible roof edges, and visible hardware provide construction detail. Model architecture and bench yourself; the fern is the only supplied model.

Create at least seven asset families, using dcc_asset_id for each definition and dcc_instance_id for each placement. All created object IDs begin ext.; preserve the four starter IDs. Include architecture, bench, terracotta containers, fern planting, useful tools/seed supplies, hardware and ground/path. Use 3–6 pot sizes and a purposeful activity grouping with some clear working surface. Pot rims have thickness, visible interiors and plausible supports. Planting is clustered by exposure/use, with varied orientation and restrained scale variation. Retain one identifiable fern mesh ext.fern.hero. Use linked mesh duplicates for repeated source variants when useful; avoid collection/Geometry Nodes instancers because their persistent identity is outside this evaluator's coverage.

Use the supplied **Poly Haven** kit for brick, timber, earth and ferns. The 2K set is common to all runs. The main facade uses material ID brick and its red_brick diffuse image in the active surface graph; bench top uses timber and weathered_brown_planks; ext.ground uses ground and forest_ground_04. Roughness and OpenGL normal maps belong in Non-Color space; albedo is sRGB. Alpha and displacement data are also Non-Color. Give the maps a plausible physical scale and keep grain aligned with construction. Do not use Normal directly as surface color or a material-preview image as albedo. The scan's physical tile estimates are 1.4 m for brick, 1.8 m for timber, 3.15 m for forest ground; check visible brick/board size and explain any adjustment.

Create shared material ID metal with node named Principled BSDF, scalar unlinked Roughness 0.38, for visible iron/steel hardware or tools. Other material controls are your design choices. Use rain effects selectively: darkened porous areas, sheltered dry patches, limited shallow reflections and damp soil. Avoid turning every surface into chrome or adding indiscriminate dirt. The photos are construction/mood evidence only; do not copy their full buildings, camera, season or palette. references/sources.json gives credits; they are private reference images, not output textures or redistributed final media.

Keep one scene, three original cameras, the original light/world, no linked libraries, no unmeasured shader node groups, at most 2,000 objects and 2.5 million evaluated triangles. Convert curves/text used as permanent visible geometry to mesh; assign unique IDs and material IDs. Retain editable separate assets rather than merging the entire environment. Pack every used FILE image and check a fresh-process reopen. Preserve all native attempts and scripts in output/; never silently replace a failed result.

Budget: 45 minutes for this build, at most 8 preview captures, two materially different repairs per requirement. Save output/pre.blend, output/CONTINUE.md, source scripts, used-asset record and actual preview/repair counts. The 1280×720, 48-sample Cycles preset is the evaluation preset. Lower-resolution provisional previews are allowed, but restore saved settings before final save. No outside assets, network research, paid generation or other scene imports. Do not spend the budget on a large framework.

Quality rubric (separate from technical acceptance): real-world scale and construction; physical material scale/response; believable vegetation/contact; purposeful composition and activity; consistency across all three views. Each is rated 1 unusable, 2 blockout, 3 coherent draft needing substantial refinement, 4 usable with minor cleanup, 5 finished for this photographic brief. Object count and technical passes alone earn no visual score.
