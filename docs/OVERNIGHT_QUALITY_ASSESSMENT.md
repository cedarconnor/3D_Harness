# Courtyard quality assessment

The accumulated revisions make this a more coherent stylized courtyard. They do not yet make it a highly detailed production environment. My assessment is **3/5 against the broader detailed-environment goal**: the composition is readable and the palette is consistent, but substantial refinement remains. Anonymous critics scored the intermediate whole scene **4/5 against a narrower stylized-diorama brief**. Those are different standards, not measurements to average.

## What improved

The open canopy has a clearer construction rhythm. The birdbath was then moved after geometry measurements showed that opening the roof alone did not reveal it. It now reads in both fixed garden and overview views. A low herb bed gives the front edge a purpose. At the bench, a loose plant, receiving pot, soil and stored supplies communicate an activity rather than a collection of unrelated props.

The final hand-tool revision adds a shaped trowel, connected fork shoulder and visibly perforated watering-can head. The selected muted steel finish separates those heads from the timber without changing the rest of the palette. The fourth anonymous critic preferred steel, then the same geometry in bronze, then unchanged. It tied all three whole-scene scores at 4/5 and rated their detail 4/4/3: the improvement is local, not evidence of a substantially better overall composition.

The restrained teal, timber, terracotta and pale masonry palette remains consistent. Lighting, cameras, inherited material graphs and unrelated objects survived the scoped continuation stages within the observer's declared coverage. These preservation results are more informative about the harness than small differences between single beauty images.

## What still limits the result

- **Detail hierarchy:** the broad paving occupies much of the frame, while the useful new activity is tiny in the overview. More objects alone will not solve this. The intended viewing distance and use need to govern asset effort.
- **Construction and vegetation:** several assets still have simplified silhouettes and repeated foliage. The very straight dark foreground soil band and regularly spaced clumps look deliberately arranged. The right pergola carries enough visual weight to compete with the entry.
- **Materials and lighting:** the warm palette is coherent, but many surfaces share a similar clean, softly lit finish. There is little reference-specific variation in weathering, fabrication, roughness or plant character. Random dirt would add noise without supplying that specificity.
- **Production coverage:** native preservation, scoped geometry checks and saved-file dependency checks are not general topology, UV, simulation, animation, export or artist-editability qualification.

## What this says about the harness

Fresh workers can continue from sealed files, preserve earlier accepted decisions, leave bounded alternatives, and hand their work to separate native evaluators and anonymous image critics. Retained failures matter: a render/viewport geometry mismatch and evaluated-UV numeric noise both produced misleading gates before they were reproduced and corrected. Tests now cover those failures, including tiny real authored UV changes that must still be rejected.

This is a useful continuity and evaluation prototype. The coordinator still performed orchestration and diagnosis in an active chat. It is not an unattended runtime, a security boundary, a replacement MCP, or evidence that the method beats plain MCP. Earlier artist preferences for plain-MCP entries remain relevant. A new held-out, balanced comparison needs a frozen target style and an artist-quality rubric before further infrastructure can be justified by outcome quality.

## The next artist decision

Choose the target finish before extending this particular scene further:

1. **More realistic, more detailed environment:** reference-led reconstruction of one hero region; stronger plant and material specificity; close-view standards; then propagate the accepted method across the scene.
2. **Refined stylized diorama:** retain the simplified language; tune focal hierarchy, reduce repetitive planting and ground patterns, and refine only the assets visible at the intended camera distance.

Either can support long-horizon evaluation. They require different modeling and material choices, so autonomous micro-polish should not silently decide between them. Artist acceptance and that direction choice remain open.
