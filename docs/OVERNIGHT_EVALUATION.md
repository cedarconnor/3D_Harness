## What the first repairs established

Opening a canopy does not necessarily reveal the object beneath it. The initial birdbath had only 21.7% sampled geometric visibility from the garden camera and 34.0% from the overview. Both canopy alternatives failed a **predeclared 55% minimum in each view**, even though a fresh anonymous critic preferred the regular open-timber structure. Remaining posts, braces and a raised bed were the measured blockers.

The next stage explicitly expanded scope to a rigid birdbath relocation. Moving it left improved the garden view but reached only 52.1% in the overview, so that candidate remains failed. A further left/forward alternative passed at **88.7% garden and 97.4% overview**. The fixed-view images were also inspected. A combined original-to-repair native comparison permits only the declared canopy transforms/deletions and birdbath translation; it passes. The workshop, original courtyard, cameras, lights and material definitions were preserved within the observer's coverage.

These are primary opaque-geometry ray samples over a projected region, not a percentage of rendered pixels or a perceptual-quality score. They identify a concrete cause and verify a bounded remedy. They do not prove recognizability, transparency behavior, exact support/contact or general artistic quality.
